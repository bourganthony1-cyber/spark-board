Privacy / How this works

  • Spark X Virality Board is a personal research desk for @BourgAnthony (StableAFdesigns) and @solitelectric. It isn't a product.
  • Watchlist posts come from read-only X API pulls. Nothing on this page posts, likes, follows, or DMs.
  • Drafts are suggestions only. Fill every [placeholder] with something true before posting.
  • No visitor data is collected: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking cookies. The theme preference stays in localStorage on your device.
  • Not affiliated with X Corp. Third-party posts belong to their authors. This page only mirrors public view counts for research.
  • View counts are a snapshot taken at pull time. Fresh posts keep climbing, and partial-pull accounts undercount.
